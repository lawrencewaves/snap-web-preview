1. Who we are and what we do

SNAP SURF TRIPS (a brand of Associació Carving's Maresme, NIF G66143009) acts as a commercial agent of surf camps and surf houses: it presents their surf trips and manages enquiries. SNAP SURF TRIPS is not a travel agency. The accommodation, surf lessons and other services are provided by independent providers, and the contract for those services is concluded between the customer and the provider.

2. Enquiries

An enquiry sent through this website is a request for information. It does not create a booking or any obligation to pay.

3. Prices

No prices are published on this website. Every trip is quoted personally and the valid price is the one stated in the written proposal.

4. Proposal, booking and payment

After your enquiry we send you a written proposal with the services included, dates, price, payment terms, cancellation terms and the identity of the provider of the services. The booking exists only when you expressly accept that proposal and make the payment it indicates. [BOOKING, PAYMENT AND CANCELLATION TERMS: TO BE COMPLETED WITH THE LEGAL ADVISER]

5. Travel requirements

Flights, transfers and travel insurance are not included unless the proposal says so. Each traveller is responsible for valid travel documents. Surfing is a physical activity with inherent risks; participants follow the instructions of the surf school.

6. Minors

Minors travel only in groups organised by a school or organisation and accompanied by responsible adults, from the age of 16.

7. Complaints

You can send any complaint to info@TU-DOMINIO.com. We will answer as soon as possible and in any case within one month.
