Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23 · The Spanish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Associació Carving's Maresme (SNAP SURF TRIPS), NIF G66143009, C/ Mossèn Cinto Verdaguer 13-15, 08329 Teià (Barcelona), Spain. Contact for privacy matters: WhatsApp +34 618 649 171 or by post to C/ Mossèn Cinto Verdaguer 13-15, 08329 Teià (Barcelona), Spain.

2. What data we collect

The data you give us in the forms: name, email, telephone, country, organisation, and the details of your trip (dates, number of people, ages of a group, surf level, needs and budget). We also record the page from which you send the enquiry, its language and, if present in the link you followed, the campaign parameters (UTM). We do not ask for special categories of data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare and manage your proposal and booking: steps prior to a contract at your request and performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To send you news and offers by email, only if you tick the optional box: your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To comply with legal obligations (accounting, tax, attending to your rights): art. 6.1.c GDPR.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a booking: up to 24 months after the last contact. Bookings: the periods required by tax and commercial law. Marketing: until you unsubscribe; after that we keep only your email on an exclusion list so that we do not write to you again.

5. Who receives your data

The accommodation and the surf school that would provide the service, only when you ask for a proposal or a booking. Providers acting on our behalf as processors: website hosting, email and office tools (Google), CRM tools (HubSpot) and AI-assisted management tools (Anthropic). Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent, by contacting us via WhatsApp +34 618 649 171 or by post to C/ Mossèn Cinto Verdaguer 13-15, 08329 Teià (Barcelona), Spain. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Minors

The forms are for adults. Trips for school groups are arranged with the school or organisation, never directly with students.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: restricted access, encrypted connections and minimisation of the data we keep.
