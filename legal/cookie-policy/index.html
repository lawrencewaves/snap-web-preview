Cookie policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16 · The Spanish version prevails.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Spanish law (art. 22.2 LSSI-CE) requires your informed consent before installing any cookie that is not strictly necessary.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website does not currently use cookies or any similar technology that requires your consent. It installs no analytics, advertising or social media cookies, loads no third-party fonts or scripts, and therefore shows no cookie banner. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first, with the same ease to accept or to reject.

3. How to manage cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies from the settings of your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Blocking them does not affect the use of this website.
