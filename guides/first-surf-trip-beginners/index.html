Your first surf trip: what to expect as a beginner

Never surfed before? Good: most people on a surf camp are beginners. This is what a first surf week usually looks like.

First, on the sand

Lessons start on the beach: how to lie on the board, paddle and stand up. Then you try it in the white water close to shore.

Sessions of 1 h 30

On the 7-night package there are 2 sessions a day. Surfing is tiring: it is normal to feel your arms and shoulders the first days.

Safety first

You should be comfortable in the water. Always follow the instructor: they choose the spot and the conditions for your level.

Progress at your pace

Some people stand up on the first day, others later. What matters is enjoying the sea and the group.
