Can you go to a surf camp on your own?

Yes, and it is common. A surf camp is built for meeting people: shared lessons, shared meals and common areas. This is how to make the most of it when you travel alone.

By Robert Lawrence, partner at SNAP SURF TRIPS · Updated 2026-11-23

Lessons are by level, not by group of friends

You are placed with people at your own level, so being alone makes no difference in the water. Tell us your real level in the enquiry: it is the most useful thing you can share.

Choose your room

The surf house in Sagres has private and shared rooms. A shared room is the easiest way to meet people; a private room gives you space to rest after a day of surf. Your proposal sets out the options.

Meeting people

Meals, the pool and the common areas are where conversations start. Say hello on the first evening: it is the easiest moment to join a table for dinner.

Choose the length

There are packages of 3, 5 and 7 nights. If it is your first time, the 7-night format with five surf days gives you the most time in the water; 3 or 5 nights suit a shorter trip.

Pack light, plan the rest

Use the packing list and remember that flights and airport transfers are not part of the packages. New to surfing? Read what to expect on your first surf trip.

How to ask

Send one enquiry from the Sagres page with your dates and level. We reply with a personalised proposal, with no commitment.
