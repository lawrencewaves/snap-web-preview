How to organise a group surf trip, step by step

Organising a surf trip for a school, a university association, a club or a company is simpler when you follow a clear order. These are the steps we follow with every group.

1. Define the group

Approximate number of people, ages and surf level. An estimate is enough to start: for example «about 30, aged 18 to 24, mostly beginners».

2. Choose the dates

In Sagres, group trips run from March to July and from mid-September to December. Give two or three options if you can: it makes availability easier.

3. Pick the length

3, 5 or 7 nights. The 7-night format includes 5 days of surf with 2 sessions of 1 h 30 a day, equipment, transport, picnic and insurance.

4. Check the rules for minors

School groups travel from age 16, organised by the school with accompanying teachers. Supervision and insurance are set out in the proposal.

5. Ask for one closed proposal

Send what you know. We check availability and send a proposal with everything included and the final price. Flights are not part of the packages, so book them once dates are confirmed.
