How to choose a surf camp: 7 things to check

Surf camps can look alike from the outside. These seven points make the difference between a good week and a disappointing one. Use them to compare any camp you are considering.

By Robert Lawrence, partner at SNAP SURF TRIPS · Updated 2026-11-27

1. Level and lessons

Lessons should be organised by level, from complete beginners to intermediate surfers. Ask how many students there are per instructor and how long each session lasts. On the 7-night surf package in Sagres, sessions last 1 h 30, twice a day.

2. Equipment

Check whether the board and the wetsuit are included in the price or rented separately, and whether the boards are suitable for your level.

3. Where you surf

Is there transport to the spot? Do the instructors choose the beach each day according to the swell and the wind? A camp that surfs the same beach whatever the conditions is a warning sign.

4. Accommodation

Private rooms, shared rooms or both; common areas; swimming pool; breakfast included or not. Choose according to whether you want to meet people or to rest after the sessions.

5. Safety and insurance

Ask what insurance is included, who supervises the sessions and what the rules are for minors. School groups travel from age 16, organised by the school.

6. Season and dates

Check that the camp is open when you want to travel and whether group dates differ from individual ones. See the month-by-month guide to Sagres.

7. A clear written price

Ask for a written proposal that lists what is included, the payment terms, the cancellation terms and who provides each service. Flights and airport transfers are usually extra: they are not part of our packages.
