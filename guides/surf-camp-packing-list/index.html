What to pack for a surf camp: the essential list

You do not need much for a surf trip, but forgetting a few things can spoil a day. This is the list we recommend.

For the water

  • Swimsuit or bikini to wear under the wetsuit
  • High-protection, water-resistant sunscreen
  • Beach towel or poncho
  • Flip-flops

Board and wetsuit: check what your package includes. On the 7-night surf package equipment is included.

For the rest of the day

  • A warm layer or hoodie for the evenings by the sea
  • Comfortable clothes and trainers
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Cap and sunglasses

Documents

  • ID card or passport
  • European Health Insurance Card if you live in the EU
  • Travel insurance details, if you have it

Leave at home

Valuables you will not use. You will spend most of the day on the beach.
