About us

Who we are

SNAP SURF TRIPS is run by two partners, Eric Arabía and Robert Lawrence, who have spent years running surf camps in the Algarve and living with surfers from all over the world. We know what a good surf week needs.

Surf destinations Contact us

What we do

We are a commercial agent for surf camps and surf houses. You send one enquiry with your dates, your group and your surf level; we check availability with the camp and send you one personalised proposal. Sending an enquiry is not a booking.

Eric Arabía · Partner

Has managed a surf camp in the Algarve, day to day, welcoming surfers from all over the world.

Robert Lawrence · Partner

Has managed a surf school in the Algarve: lessons, levels and safety on the water.

How we work

  • Surfers first: we match the camp to your level and your group.
  • Honest proposals: one closed proposal with what is included. No commitment until you accept it.
  • Camps we know: we only offer surf camps we work with directly.
  • Active life and nature: surf is sport, sea and friends in the same week.
  • One contact: from your first email to the last wave.

Who is behind SNAP SURF TRIPS

SNAP SURF TRIPS is a brand of Associació Carving's Maresme, based in Teià (Barcelona), Spain. Legal details are in the legal notice.
