Group surf trips · Universities

Surf trips for universities & Erasmus

For student associations, university sports services and Erasmus groups: a surf week in Sagres with accommodation, lessons and transport in one closed proposal.

Request a group proposal

Built for student groups

Shared rooms, common areas and a pool: the formula that works for big student groups. One organiser talks to one contact.

Mixed levels, no problem

Beginners and surfers who already ride waves can travel together: lessons are organised by level.

The destination

Sagres, at the south-western tip of the Algarve, Portugal. Your group stays in a surf house with a swimming pool, private and shared rooms, common areas and its own surf school.

What a group trip can include

  • Accommodation with breakfast
  • Surf lessons for every level, with equipment
  • Transport to the surf spot
  • Picnic and insurance on the 7-night format
  • Yoga and other activities on request

Season and group size

Group trips run from March to July and from mid-September to December. Group proposals start at 20 people. Fewer than 20? Send your enquiry from the Sagres page.

How it works

You send what you know. We check availability with the accommodation and the surf school and send one closed proposal with the final price. One contact from the first email to the last wave.

Questions

How big can the group be?

Group proposals start at 20 people. Tell us your approximate number and we check availability.

Can each student pay separately?

Payment terms are set out in the proposal. Tell us how your group prefers to pay when you enquire.

Are flights included?

No. Flights and airport transfers are not part of the packages.
