Surf & work trip
to Bali

A small boutique stay with its own coworking space on the Bukit Peninsula, a short drive from the reef breaks around Uluwatu. Surf in the morning, work with fast wifi in the afternoon. For 1 to 18 people, all year round, quoted for your dates.

All year round

Warm water,
twelve months

Open all year for 1 to 18 people: solo, with friends or as a small team. The Bukit is at its best in the dry season (April to October); in the wet season we point you to the breaks that work best.

Reef country

~15 min

Balangan

Long, rippable lefts in front of a sandy beach. A favourite for a sunset session.

Bali's most famous barrel on the big days, with a gentler inside section when it is smaller.
