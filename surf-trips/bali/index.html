Surf trip
to Bali

A small boutique surf & coworking hotel on the Bukit peninsula, in the Uluwatu area: surf in the morning, work or rest in the afternoon. Open all year, for travellers and small groups up to 20.

The stay

Surf & remote work

A boutique hotel with few rooms, personal service and a coworking space. The surf spots of the Uluwatu area, like Balangan, Padang Padang and Bingin, are a short drive away. Bali airport is about 30 minutes by car.

  • Swimming pool
  • Breakfast included
  • Coworking with fast wifi
  • Spa & massages
  • Rooms with balcony or terrace
Get my proposal Group proposal

Who it is for

Surfers who want to combine waves and remote work, friends and small groups of up to 20 people. Adults only.

When to go

Open all year. Tell us your dates and we check availability.

Stay, coworking and surf

You stay and work at the hotel, which has the coworking space. Surf lessons are given by a collaborating surf school outside the hotel. Tell us your level: we confirm the surf options, dates and what is included in your personalised proposal.

How it works

One enquiry, one proposal with the final price. Flights and airport transfers are not included unless the proposal says so.
