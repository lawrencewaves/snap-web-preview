Surf trip FAQ: prices, packages and bookings

Questions,
answered

What is SNAP SURF TRIPS?

SNAP SURF TRIPS is a commercial agent for surf camps and surf houses around the world, starting in Sagres, Portugal. We present their surf trips to individual travellers and groups and manage your enquiry: you send one request, we check availability with the accommodation and the surf school, and you receive one personalised proposal. The services are provided by the accommodation and the surf school.

Is sending an enquiry a booking?

No. An enquiry only asks for availability and price. Nothing is booked or charged until you accept the proposal we send you.

How much does a surf trip to Sagres cost?

Every trip is quoted personally. The price depends on your dates, room type, number of people and surf days. Send an enquiry and you receive a proposal with the exact price, with no commitment.

What is included in the surf packages?

The 3-night package includes 3 breakfasts, 1 day of surf and transport to the spot. The 5-night package includes 5 breakfasts, 2 days of surf and transport. The 7-night package includes 7 breakfasts, 5 days of surf with 2 sessions of 1 h 30 a day, transport, equipment, picnic and insurance. Flights and airport transfers are not part of the packages.

When can I go?

Sagres: the surf house is open from March to December. Bali: open all year.

Where exactly is the accommodation?

In the destination you choose: Sagres, in the Algarve (Portugal), or the Bukit peninsula in Bali (Indonesia). You receive the exact details together with your proposal and booking confirmation.

Do you organise surf trips for groups?

Yes. Together with the surf camp we prepare closed proposals for schools, universities and Erasmus groups, clubs and associations, private groups and companies. Group prices are always quoted on request. School groups are for students aged 16 and over and are organised through the school.

How do I pay?

Payment terms are detailed in your proposal before you confirm anything.
